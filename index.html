Arduino-Based Motion Tracking Radar System

Designed and implemented an Arduino-based motion tracking radar system capable of detecting objects and monitoring their distance and direction.

The system uses an ultrasonic sensor mounted on a servo motor to scan its surroundings. Detection information can be presented through the connected output components. The project was also developed with a Processing-based GUI for real-time visualization.
